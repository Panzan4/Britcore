        How to do England

        The unwritten rules, made simple.

        Driving on the left

        The basics: Keep left and overtake on the right. Speed limits are in mph. Check your licence is valid for visiting drivers before you drive.

        Roundabouts: Go clockwise and give way to traffic coming from your right. Use the left lane to turn left or go straight on, and the right lane to turn right. Signal left after you pass the exit before yours.

        Zebra crossings: Give way to people waiting to cross.

        Motorways: Keep left unless overtaking. A red X above a lane means it is closed, so never drive in it. Overhead speed limits in a red circle must be obeyed.

        Country lanes: Many are single track with passing places. Pull into a passing place on your left, or wait opposite one on your right, and never park in one. Slow down for blind bends and pass horses slowly with plenty of room.

        London: There is a congestion charge and an ultra low emission zone, so check TfL before you drive in.

        Nearby essentials

        Tap to search near you in your maps app.

        Pharmacies: Look for Boots or Superdrug. Supermarkets: Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Morrisons, Co-op and M&S Food. Large shops can only open for six hours on Sundays, while small local branches often stay open longer.

        Where am I?

        A postcode cannot be worked out here. If you can see one on a sign or building, give that too, along with street names or landmarks. 112 also works. Many phones share your location automatically on 999 calls, but always tell the operator where you are.
